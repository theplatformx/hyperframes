# StudioX Viewport · TradingView motion study

## Intent

- Thirty-second 16:9, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps independent concept film.
- TradingView is the subject. StudioX Viewport is the creator credit; StudioX has no space.
- A coded moving market signal drives the action. Motion is continuous and causally linked across cuts. No screenshot pan or static image montage.
- Premium editorial type, energetic but readable. Closing phrase: “Great ideas should move faster.”
- Code-driven charts and UI are clearly illustrative, not current prices, financial advice, or an official TradingView promotion.
- Full music and sound mix, with prominent accents tied to authored visual beats.

## Production choice

Follow the signal from macro data into a chart, through an illustrative chart interaction, and out through kinetic typography. The source remains editable HTML/CSS/SVG/GSAP; Hyperframes captures frames and mixes audio. The previous cut is reference for brand copy only, not a motion template.

## Scope

One finished MP4, source, storyboard, audio cues, reproducible build and checks. Existing fork and PR remain intact; this is a new project directory.
